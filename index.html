About

I am a final-year undergraduate in Mathematics and Applied Mathematics (Innovation Class) at Xinjiang University, ranked 1st of 134 in my major. Advised by Prof. Cheng Hu at the School of Mathematics and System Sciences, I study stability and synchronization of stochastic networked systems: how to steer noisy, multilayer networks onto a common trajectory within a guaranteed time, using control that is switched on only intermittently, sends quantized signals, and must tolerate deception attacks.

This work began with a National Undergraduate Innovation Training Program project that I lead, published as [J1], and continues with Prof. Hu in [J2] and [J3]. I am now also exploring a data-driven version of the problem: synchronizing stochastic networks whose dynamics and couplings are known only through discrete snapshots.

Research interests

  • Fixed- and prescribed-time synchronization of stochastic multilayer networks
  • Intermittent, quantized and self-triggered control under deception attacks
  • Learning-assisted control with finite-sample guarantees
  • Data-driven modeling and control of complex systems

Research experience

Advisor: Prof. Cheng Hu, School of Mathematics and System Sciences, Xinjiang University. For [J1]–[J3], theory, Matlab/SDE validation and manuscript writing were done independently.

Finite-time synchronization control of stochastic multilayer networks

Mar 2025 – Dec 2026

Principal Investigator, National Undergraduate Innovation Training Program (No. 202510755121)

Proposed a fuzzy intermittent quantized control scheme whose L2-type Lyapunov function removes the Jensen-inequality bottleneck of existing results, and relaxed strong connectivity by embedding the target orbit as a virtual node. Obtained fixed- and prescribed-time synchronization under deception attacks, with an image-encryption application.

Published as [J1]

Generalized aperiodic intermittent synchronization of stochastic multilayer networks

Jan 2026 – present

Sole student researcher

Designed a partial-convexity interpolation mechanism that recycles the sublinear terms discarded by conventional estimates, and proved existence and uniqueness of the optimal threshold by nested parameter optimization. The result is an unconditionally tighter fixed-time bound, with the design extended from periodic to general aperiodic regimes.

Manuscript [J2]

Learning-assisted dual self-triggered intermittent quantized control of stochastic systems

Mar 2026 – present

Sole student researcher

Established a bounded-deficit stability framework and a learning-assisted dual self-triggered controller. Dual MLPs learn the conditional mean and variance of open-loop state energy to adapt work/rest durations; trajectory-level split conformal calibration supplies a finite-sample excursion envelope; and a pathwise credit ceiling guarantees mean-square exponential stability under deception attacks.

Manuscript [J3]

Data-driven finite-time synchronization of stochastic networks with unknown dynamics

2026 – present

Ongoing idea

For stochastic sub-networks whose dynamics and interactions are unknown and observed only through discrete snapshots, the aim is to learn a simulatable deep-learning surrogate and design robustly compensated controllers that still achieve finite-time synchronization.
